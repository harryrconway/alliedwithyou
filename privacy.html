Privacy

How we collect, use, store and share your personal and health information.

What we collect

To provide occupational therapy, we collect information such as your name, contact details, date of birth, funding details, information about your health and daily life, and the details of people involved in your care. We only collect what we need.

How we collect it

Mostly from you directly, during conversations, assessments and appointments. With your consent, we may also collect information from family, carers, your GP, other health professionals, your plan manager or support coordinator.

Why we collect it

We use your information to provide and plan your therapy, write reports you've asked for, communicate with people involved in your care when you've agreed to it, manage billing and meet our legal and professional obligations.

Who we share it with

We only share your information with your consent, or when the law requires or allows it, for example to prevent a serious threat to someone's safety. We never sell your information or use it for marketing.

How we store and protect it

Records are kept securely, with access limited to the people who need it to provide your care. We keep health records for the periods required by law, then dispose of them securely.

Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask to see the information we hold about you and ask us to correct anything that's wrong. Contact us and we'll respond within a reasonable time, usually 30 days.

This website

This website doesn't use analytics or tracking cookies. When you contact us through the website, your message is sent to our practice email and isn't stored on the website. The referral form is provided by Splose, the system we use to manage client records, so a referral made through it goes straight into Splose and is stored there securely.

If you start filling in a form, what you type is kept in that browser tab until you send it or close the tab, so it isn't lost if the page reloads. It stays on your device and isn't sent anywhere until you choose to send it. If you change the text size or contrast, that choice is remembered in your browser for next time.

Questions and complaints

If you have a question or concern about how we handle your information, please contact us first. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. New South Wales, Victoria and the ACT also have their own health records laws, so you can also contact the Information and Privacy Commission NSW, the Health Complaints Commissioner in Victoria, or the ACT Human Rights Commission.
