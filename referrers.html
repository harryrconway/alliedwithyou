Information for referrers

For GPs, support coordinators, plan managers, aged care providers, hospitals and other health professionals. Everything you need to refer someone to us, on one page you can print.

Make a referral

How to refer

Use the online referral form, or email hello@alliedwithyou.com.au. Please make sure the person knows about the referral and is happy for us to contact them. For NDIS participants, let us know how their plan is managed.

What to include

Only what we need to get in touch and understand the request: contact details, a sentence or two about why you're referring, and how therapy will be funded.

Please leave out detailed medical history. If you have reports, plans or letters to share, we'll send you a secure way to provide them once we've made contact. Please don't attach them to an ordinary email.

What happens next

We'll contact the person, or their representative, to talk about their needs, how sessions will be funded and when we can start. We'll let you know once we've made contact. If there's a wait to start, we'll say so.

If we can't help, for example because of where they live or how their plan is managed, we'll tell you and suggest other options.

Funding we work with

  • NDIS plans that are self managed or plan managed
  • Support at Home, invoiced to the provider at the rate agreed with them
  • Medicare GP plans: a GP referral under a chronic condition management plan is needed for the rebate
  • Private clients, including those claiming from a health fund

Reports

If a funding body, doctor or other professional needs information from us, we write it up clearly and check it with the person before it's sent. With their consent, we can send you a copy.
