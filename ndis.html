Occupational therapy for NDIS participants

We work with NDIS participants across the Northern Rivers and Gold Coast, and by telehealth anywhere in Australia. Here is how it works with us.

How occupational therapy fits into your plan

Occupational therapy is usually funded from the Capacity Building budget in your plan, under Improved Daily Living. That covers assessments, therapy sessions, report writing and, where it applies, travel.

Equipment and home modifications are usually funded separately, from the Capital budget. We can write the recommendations and quotes the NDIS asks for when you need these.

What we can help with

  • Functional capacity assessments that describe your support needs clearly
  • Therapy to build skills and independence with everyday tasks
  • Assistive technology and home modification recommendations
  • Progress reports ahead of a plan reassessment
  • Advice for support workers and families on safe, practical ways to help

Before we start

We'll give you a service agreement that sets out the supports we'll provide, the rates, how invoices are handled and our cancellation terms. We go through it with you, and you can ask your plan manager or support coordinator to look at it too.

We'll also ask for your consent to collect and share information, for example with your plan manager or support coordinator.

What we charge

We charge in line with the NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits for therapy supports, including travel and report writing where they apply. The exact rates are listed in your service agreement, and we'll always explain them before we begin. See fees and cancellations for more.

Invoices

If your plan is plan managed, we send invoices straight to your plan manager. If you self manage, we send them to you to claim through the my NDIS portal or app.

Reports for plan reassessments

If you have a plan reassessment coming up, let us know as soon as you have a date. A useful progress report takes time to prepare, and we'd rather not rush it. We'll talk about what the report needs to cover and check it with you before it goes anywhere.

For support coordinators

You're welcome to send us a referral online or by email. We'll contact the participant to talk about their goals, confirm their plan details and send a service agreement. Make a referral.

Using your plan with us

Tell us how your plan is managed when you get in touch, and we'll explain how invoicing will work for you.
