About Allied With You

A small, local occupational therapy practice that works alongside the people we see, their families and the professionals around them.

Who we are

Allied With You is an occupational therapy practice working with people across the Northern Rivers and the Gold Coast, and by telehealth anywhere in Australia.

We help people manage everyday tasks, stay safe at home and keep doing the things that matter to them. That might mean building skills after an illness or injury, finding the right equipment, making changes around the home, or writing a clear report so the right support can be funded.

Most of our work happens where life happens: in your home, out in your community, or by telehealth when that suits better.

Who we work with

We see people paying privately, people using a health fund, Medicare GP plan or Support at Home package, and NDIS participants. Family members, carers, GPs and support coordinators are welcome to get in touch on someone's behalf.

How we work

We listen first
The first part of any work together is understanding your life: your routines, the people around you, what you enjoy and what's become difficult. Goals are set in your words, not ours.
We work where life happens
A kitchen, a bathroom, the bus stop down the road. Seeing how things work in real places makes our advice more useful and easier to put into practice.
We keep it practical
Recommendations should be things you can actually do, afford and keep using. If something isn't working, we change it.
We work with your team
With your permission, we talk to family, carers, GPs, support coordinators and other therapists, so everyone is working towards the same thing.
We're upfront about costs
You'll know what something costs before it happens, and what a report or assessment is for before we start writing it.

Want to talk it through?

If you're wondering whether occupational therapy could help, send us a message. There's no need to have it all worked out first.
