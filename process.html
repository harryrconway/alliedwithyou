From first contact to finishing up

Everyone's path looks a little different. Some people need a single home visit. Others work with us for months.

  1. Getting in touch

    You can contact us yourself, or a family member, GP, support coordinator or other professional can refer you. Email us or get in touch through the website, whatever's easiest.

  2. A conversation first

    We'll get in touch to talk about what's happening, what you'd like help with and how sessions will be funded. If we're not the right fit, we'll say so and suggest other options.

  3. Paperwork, kept simple

    Before the first appointment we send a short service agreement and consent form. They explain what we'll do, what it costs and how your information is handled. Questions are always welcome.

  4. Assessment

    At the first appointment we get to know you and how things are going day to day. We look at what's working, what isn't, and what you'd like to be different.

  5. Setting goals

    Together we agree on goals, written in your words, and a plan for working towards them.

  6. Therapy and recommendations

    Depending on your goals, this might be regular sessions, equipment trials, changes around the home, or advice for the people who support you.

  7. Reports, when they're needed

    If a funding body, doctor or other professional needs information from us, we write it up clearly and check it with you before it's sent.

  8. Reviewing and finishing up

    We check progress regularly. When you've reached your goals, we plan to finish together and leave you with what you need to keep going. You're welcome back any time.

Where we see people

We work wherever makes the most sense for what you're working on.

  • At home

    Most of our work happens where you live, so advice suits your space and your routine.

  • In the community

    At the shops, on public transport, at work or anywhere else you want to feel more confident.

  • By telehealth

    Video sessions for check ins, reviews and conversations that don't need us in the room, wherever you live in Australia.

Waiting times

If there's a wait to start, we'll tell you when you first get in touch.

We'd rather be honest about timing than book you in and push appointments back. If your situation is urgent, let us know and we'll do our best to help or point you to someone who can.
