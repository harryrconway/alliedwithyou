Easy Read

This page tells you about Allied With You in an easy way.

Who we are

  • We are Allied With You.

  • We are occupational therapists.

  • We help people do everyday things.

What we help with

  • Getting showered and dressed.

  • Moving around your home safely.

  • Cooking, shopping and getting out in your community.

  • Finding equipment that makes things easier, like a shower chair.

  • Writing reports for the NDIS or aged care.

Where we see you

  • At your home.

  • Out in your community.

  • On a video call. This is called telehealth.

  • We visit people in the Northern Rivers and on the Gold Coast.

  • We can do video calls with people anywhere in Australia.

Who you will see

  • You will see Jake or Chelsie.

  • Jake and Chelsie own Allied With You.

  • You can tell us who you would like to see.

Paying

  • You can pay yourself.

  • You can use your NDIS plan if it is self managed or plan managed.

  • You can use Support at Home from My Aged Care.

  • You might get some money back from Medicare or your health fund.

  • We will tell you the cost before we start.

How to start

  • Send us an email: hello@alliedwithyou.com.au

  • Or fill in the form on our website.

  • Someone can contact us for you, like your family, your doctor or your support coordinator.

  • You do not need a letter from your doctor if you pay yourself.

Help to talk with us

  • We can get an interpreter for you. Tell us your language.

  • You can bring a support person.

  • We can give you information in big print.
