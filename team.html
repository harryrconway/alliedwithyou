Our team

Allied With You is owned and run by Jake and Chelsie, its two principal occupational therapists. When you work with us, you work with one of them.

  • Jake

    Principal Occupational Therapist

    Owner, Allied With You

    jake@alliedwithyou.com.au

    Jake is an Occupational Therapist with extensive experience across the public and private health sectors.

    His background includes neurological and complex rehabilitation, working with brain injuries, spinal cord injuries and other neurological conditions. Jake also has extensive NDIS experience, including functional capacity assessments, home modifications (minor and complex) and equipment prescription.

    He takes a practical, person-centred approach, working with clients, families and support teams to find solutions that last.

    Ask to see Jake

  • Chelsie

    Principal Occupational Therapist

    Owner, Allied With You

    chelsie@alliedwithyou.com.au

    Chelsie is an Occupational Therapist with experience across both private practice and public health, helping people regain independence at home and in their daily lives. She began her career in the NDIS and has built a strong rehabilitation background, including inpatient rehab, extensive hand therapy experience and in-home rehabilitation with the transition aged care team.

    Calm and understanding, Chelsie takes the time to learn what matters to each client. She builds therapy around their goals, routines and environment, and her thoughtful, hands-on approach helps people feel supported at every step.

    Ask to see Chelsie

Occupational therapists in Australia are registered with the Occupational Therapy Board of Australia through Ahpra. You can check any practitioner on the public register of practitioners.

Choosing who you see

If you'd like to see Jake or Chelsie in particular, say so when you get in touch and we'll do our best to arrange it. Sometimes it depends on where you live, the kind of help you need and when you're free, so we'll tell you if the other is a better fit or could see you sooner.

Not sure? That's fine. Tell us a little about what's going on, and we'll suggest who would suit you best.
