Accessibility

We want our website and our services to be easy for everyone to use. If something gets in your way, please tell us.

This website

We're aiming for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2 at level AA. That includes:

  • Working with a keyboard alone, with a clear outline showing where you are on the page
  • Text that can be enlarged without breaking the layout
  • Colours with strong contrast, and information that never relies on colour alone
  • Plain language, clear headings and descriptive links
  • Forms with clear labels and helpful error messages

If you find something that doesn't work well for you, please let us know and we'll fix it.

Information in another format

We can provide information, service agreements and reports in larger print or another format. Just ask.

Interpreters and communication support

If you'd like an interpreter, we can arrange one through the Translating and Interpreting Service. If you're deaf, or have a hearing or speech impairment, you can contact us through the National Relay Service.

Appointments

Most of our appointments happen in your home or community, so you don't need to travel. If you'd like a support person with you, or need appointments arranged a particular way, tell us and we'll work around it.
