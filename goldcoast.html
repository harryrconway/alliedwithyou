Occupational therapy on the Gold Coast

We see people at home and out in the community across the Gold Coast, helping with everyday tasks, safety at home, equipment and the reports that get support funded.

Where we see you

Most of our work happens where you live, so advice suits your space and your routine. We come to your home across the Gold Coast, and meet you out in the community too: at the shops, on public transport, at work or anywhere else you want to feel more confident.

Some appointments work just as well by video, such as check ins, reviews and conversations that don't need us in the room.

What we can help with

  • Functional capacity assessments that describe your support needs clearly
  • Daily living and independence, from showering and dressing to cooking and shopping
  • Home safety and access, including falls risks, bathrooms, steps and layout
  • Assistive technology and equipment advice, trials and prescriptions
  • Goal focused therapy and reviews
  • Reports and recommendations for funding bodies, doctors and other professionals

Paying for therapy

We see people on the Gold Coast who pay privately, people claiming from a health fund or a Medicare GP plan, people with a Support at Home package through My Aged Care, and NDIS participants. Read more on fees and cancellations, the NDIS and My Aged Care.

Travel

For private clients, the first 20 minutes of travel each way is included. Travel beyond that is charged per hour, and we'll tell you what to expect before your first visit. For NDIS participants, travel is charged in line with the NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits.

For referrers

GPs, support coordinators, aged care providers and other health professionals on the Gold Coast can send us a referral online or by email. We'll contact the person to talk about their goals and how their therapy will be funded. Make a referral.

Get started on the Gold Coast

Send us a message, or ask your GP, support coordinator or aged care provider to refer you. If there's a wait to start, we'll tell you when you first get in touch.
