Occupational therapy services

Practical help with the things you need and want to do each day. Most people use more than one of these over time, and we'll talk through what makes sense for you.

  • Functional capacity assessments

    A thorough look at how you manage personal care, household tasks, getting around and taking part in your community, written up with clear recommendations.

  • Daily living and independence

    Building the skills, routines and confidence to manage everyday tasks your way, from showering and dressing to cooking and shopping.

  • Home safety and access

    A home visit to look at falls risks, bathroom access, steps and layout, with practical recommendations and support through any changes.

  • Assistive technology and equipment

    Finding equipment that suits you, trialling it where we can, writing funding requests and making sure it's set up properly once it arrives.

  • Goal focused therapy and reviews

    Regular sessions working on goals that matter to you, with progress checked along the way and plans adjusted when things change.

  • Reports and recommendations

    Clear, honest reports for the NDIS, aged care providers, insurers, GPs and other professionals, explaining what you need and why.
